Before you sweep, spray or bag: safety steps

General information only, taken from federal public health pages, and your own doctor outranks it on health questions.

  • Pellets can look harmless and still crumble into powder, and powder is what gets inhaled, so leave it unswept.
  • A garage, shed or crawlspace that has been shut for a while needs roughly thirty minutes of outside air before you enter.
  • Germs are too small to see, so gloves go on before you start and soap and water finish the job.
  • Wet everything thoroughly, give the product around five minutes, and lift the waste afterward with towels you plan to throw away.
  • Bag, knot, then bin. A carcass is never handled unprotected.
  • Body aches, a fever or labored breath after contact with waste are matters for a clinic and not a pest company.
