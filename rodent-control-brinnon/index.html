Safety steps for waste and nests

Agencies agree on the main points of cleanup, so those shared points are gathered here in brief.

  • Whatever the mess looks like, resist dry methods and start with water or disinfectant.
  • A sealed outbuilding should not be entered cold, so let a breeze run through for about thirty minutes.
  • Germs are too small to see, so gloves go on before you start and soap and water finish the job.
  • Disinfect before collecting. The wet waste should sit around five minutes first.
  • Bag, knot, then bin. A carcass is never handled unprotected.
  • If a flu-like illness or shortness of breath follows exposure, a physician should hear about the cleanup and make the diagnosis.

Where the safety advice comes from
