Garages, crawlspaces and shared lanes.

An attached garage ties into the house through pipes and wires, and every penetration can be a route. The technician inspects the door seal, the threshold and each pipe that crosses the shared wall.

Early signs described simply.

Here is what to watch and listen for. One clue proves little, but two or three together rarely mislead.

  • Tapping or gnawing from a soffit at night.
  • Insulation tugged out around a hatch edge.
  • Droppings on a garage shelf of totes.
  • A burning smell when you start a parked vehicle.
  • Rub marks along a fence rail or pipe.
