Questions about coverage.

What if my ZIP code is not on the list?

Phone with the street address. Because coverage follows ZIP code, every address is checked before a booking, with no guessing.

Why does Seattle list only three ZIP codes?

Seattle is a very large city, and only three of its ZIP codes fall inside our range. Its page says so, and a Seattle address with a different code needs a call to check.

Are Brinnon and Quilcene in Kitsap County?

No. Both are in Jefferson County, and Seattle is in King County. They appear here because their ZIP codes sit within about fifteen miles of Silverdale.

Is the footer address an office?

No. It is a citation address only, and nobody is stationed there. Every job is booked by phone and done at your property.
