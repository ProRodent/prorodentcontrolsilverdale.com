Before you sweep, spray or bag: safety steps

This list repeats federal advice in everyday words, and it holds for any household whether or not it ever hires us.

  • A shop vacuum feels efficient but can blow tiny particles right back into the room.
  • Air the space first. Open whatever opens, then wait about thirty minutes.
  • Start by protecting your hands and finish with a thorough scrub.
  • Think of three beats, soak then pause then wipe, where the pause lasts around five minutes.
  • A single well-tied bag is enough to carry pellets, wipes and a carcass out of the house.
  • A doctor alone can judge the seriousness of an exposure, so call one if you develop fever or labored breathing.
