Bainbridge Island, a city on an island.

Bainbridge Island is both an island and an incorporated city in Kitsap County. The city incorporated as Bainbridge Island in 1991, and an earlier city, Winslow, had been incorporated in 1947. Our data lists two ZIP codes for it, 98110 and 98061, and the second is the one used for Rolling Bay. A history like that helps you name your location on the phone, while the rodent plan itself still starts at your roofline. Safety guidance from the CDC cleanup page

Federal health agencies offer every home the same guidance, and none of it relies on hiring a company.

  • Put away the compressed air, the broom and the dry rag whenever you find pellets or stained nesting.
  • Prop open windows and doors, step outside for around thirty minutes, and let the air exchange.
  • Keep your hands covered for the whole task, then scrub.
  • Cover pellets and the surface under them with disinfectant, hold off for about five minutes, and wipe up with throwaway towels.
  • A single well-tied bag is enough to carry pellets, wipes and a carcass out of the house.
  • A doctor alone can judge the seriousness of an exposure, so call one if you develop fever or labored breathing.
