How one job leads to the next.

A typical job covers five stages: inspection, then trapping, then sealing, then cleanup, then repair. Cleanup is last on purpose, because an attic scrubbed with animals still coming and going would only get dirty again.

Many customers need just one step. An owner who already closed every gap might want cleanup only, and a shopkeeper might want monitoring and nothing more. Say what is finished, and the visit handles whatever is left.

Emergencies get no separate tier here, since urgency does not alter the work. Phones are answered through the night, and any page on this site can lead to a same-day request if there is room.

Where the safety advice comes from

The full recommended steps and product rules sit on these public pages.

Service questions, with answers.

Which page should I start with?

Not sure what you have? Begin with an inspection. It identifies the animal, locates every entry and gives you a plan before any bigger commitment.

Can I book just sealing, or just cleanup?

Yes. Tell us what you have already done, and we build the visit around what remains.

Do vehicles and yards count?

Yes. Cars and trucks get a page on engine bays and cabin vents, while yards get a page on burrows, sheds, shrubs and feeders.

Which page suits a shop or an office?

Stores, offices and cafes will want the commercial page, which explains planned checks, treatment and the notes left at each stop.
